Many of the projects recovered or continued development with bug fixes. It is likely that development was complete, and bugs were only just found during the gap. As a result, the inactivity period ends in order to address newly found issues. Since the commits were from WOC, there were some projects who had many more commits since WOC was updated.

Our discussion improved activity pattern classification from the graph. For these 10 projects, some of the activity patterns were more difficult to classify. Sometimes if you look at the changes across multiple different frames of time then it would fall into categories like irregular or cyclical, but if you look at it as a whole graph, comparing beginning and end, it could be described as declined as well. This indicates that aggregation of data like this can be reliant on personal interpretation so should be taken with caution.
